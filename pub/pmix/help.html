Practice Mix

How to use it

Practice Mix lets you listen to your choir's practice tracks and mix them: bring your own voice part forward and keep the other parts quieter in the background.

Open Practice Mix

1Open the app

It opens on the current season folder. Tap a song's folder to go in.

The buttons at the top right:

  • 📂Folder opens the CURRENT CHOIR SEASON folder in Google Drive.
  • 🔗Links opens Kathryn's helpful Links to Learn By document.
  • 📅Calendar opens the Choir Schedule with upcoming choir dates and activities.

2Pick your tracks

Tap your own part first. The first track you select is your focus track and plays at normal level.

Then tap the other parts or the accompaniment you want to sing against. These are background tracks and play quieter.

3Play Selected

Tap ▶ Play Selected to open the mixer, then practise:

  • Speed from 0.7x to 1.3x, with the pitch preserved
  • Level and pan for each track
  • Solo or mute any part
  • Repeat with the Loop switch
  • Export your mix as a WAV file

Tips

  • The app remembers your part in this browser, so next time it is ready first.
  • Tracks are saved on your device, so return visits load fast.
  • Works on a phone or a computer in a modern browser. Nothing to install.
