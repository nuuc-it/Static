Mark up a score

How to make a score follow the music

A score follows the music because of marks: numbered points on the page, each tied to a time in the recording. Editing is only needed when a score has no marks or they need fixing. Saving for everyone needs a signed-in editor; anyone else's edits stay on their own device.

Start editing

  1. Open the score (music-note button) and tap the pencil (✎) at the upper right of the score. The edit bar appears above it.
  2. Tap Done when finished.
  3. Made a mistake? Undo steps back. The ⋯ menu holds Clear all (deletes every mark), Set track offset and the Reaction slider.
  4. The strip under the bar shows PREV, NOW and NEXT: the marks either side of the playhead, named by their measure label (or #number), with the time to each. Tap PREV or NEXT to go to exactly the mark it shows (in Edit it also selects that mark), or tap the time in the middle to pick any timed mark from a list (Escape or a tap outside closes it).

Add marks

Choose what a tap adds, then tap the score where it goes.

  1. + Timed: a mark at the playhead's time. Play the music and tap at the start of each line or bar; the mark lands a little before your tap (the Reaction setting).
  2. + Untimed: a mark with no time yet. Time it later with Set to now.
  3. + EOL: an end-of-line mark. The sideways Ribbon cuts the line there.
  4. + Note: a text note. Singers see it when they tap it.
  5. + Jump: a repeat or skip. It points to a timed mark; with Edit off, tapping it goes to that mark.

Untimed, EOL, note and jump marks never affect Follow or Prev/Next.

The mark editor

Tap a mark to select it. The editor under the strip shows its measure label, which you can type right there, with -0.1, Set to now and +0.1. Selecting a mark does not move the playhead. Tap the mark again, or the arrow at the right, to open the rest, including Go to, which moves the playhead to the mark.

  1. Type: Mark, End of line, Jump or Note.
  2. Time: -0.1 s and +0.1 s nudge it; Set to now sets it to the playhead; Untime removes it.
  3. Measure label: a name such as 12. Each label can be used once.
  4. Note: text shown when a singer taps the mark.
  5. Delete removes the mark. Tap the arrow again to close the rest.

Timing and moving about

  1. Skip buttons: in Edit the transport also has -1s, +1s, -0.1s and +0.1s, beside -5s and +5s, for fine positioning.
  2. Return (|◀): left of -5s, it goes back to exactly where you last pressed Play, playing or paused. Handy for trying a start time again.
  3. Set to now: sets the selected mark to the playhead time, then selects the next mark.
  4. Cursor here: after you drag the score away while paused, tap Cursor here (upper right) to move the cursor to the point you are looking at. On the ribbon it is the point under the cursor line; on the page it is the nearest mark to the top-left. Go to beside Set to now does the same for the selected mark.
  5. Resume follow: in Edit, dragging the score pauses Follow; tap Resume follow at the upper right of the score to bring it back.
  6. PREV / NEXT: in Edit they go exactly to the mark, with no Pre-roll.

Save, Revert and Keep mine

  1. Save sends your marks to Drive for every singer. It appears when you have unsaved edits, the music is paused and you are signed in.
  2. The bar says what changed, for example Unsaved: 2 added, 3 retimed, 1 moved, ribbon height; on a computer, hover over it to see which marks. Scrolling the ribbon up or down does not count as a change; it is saved along with your next Save. Revert also brings back the saved Ribbon height and position.
  3. If someone saved a newer version first, Save is refused. Tap Revert to take the saved marks, or Keep mine to save yours over them.

Set track offset

Each voice sits at a different height below the marks. Open the ⋯ menu and tap Set track offset, choose the track, then tap where that voice's staff is under a line. Follow then keeps your focus track's staff in view. Starting playback turns the tool off.
