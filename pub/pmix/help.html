Practice Mix

How to use it

Practice Mix lets you listen to your choir's practice tracks and mix them: bring your own voice part forward and keep the other parts quieter in the background.

Open Practice Mix

1Open the app

It opens on the current season folder. Tap a song's folder to go in.

The buttons at the top right:

  • 📂Folder opens the CURRENT CHOIR SEASON folder in Google Drive.
  • 🔗Links opens Kathryn's helpful Links to Learn By document.
  • 📅Calendar opens the Choir Schedule with upcoming choir dates and activities.

2Pick your tracks

Tap your own part first. The first track you select is your focus track and plays at normal level.

Then tap the other parts or the accompaniment you want to sing against. These are background tracks and play quieter.

3Play Selected

Tap ▶ Play Selected to open the mixer, then practise:

  • Speed from 0.7x to 1.3x, with the pitch preserved
  • Level and pan for each track
  • Solo or mute any part
  • Repeat with the Loop switch
  • Export your mix as a WAV file

4Pitch Pipe

Need your starting note? Tap the Pitch Pipe button, a green tile marked PITCH . It sits at the far left of the row with Open New and Play Selected on the song page, and at the far left of the speed row on the mixer.

  • It opens on your part, or on the last part you used, or on Alto the first time. Change part with the S A T B buttons.
  • Tap a note to hear it. Hold it to keep it sounding. Use the flat, natural or sharp button first for a note in between.
  • It plays over the mix without stopping it. Close the sheet when you are done.

5Follow the score

If the song has a score that moves with the music, a score button appears on the mixer page: a navy disc with a cream rim showing a page of music . (On the song page, the PDF button opens the Drive viewer instead.) Tap it and the score opens over the playing mix. Press Play and the score follows the music.

  • Follow: the page moves with the music and the line being sung sits near the top.
  • Start from a numbered mark: tap it and the music starts exactly there.
  • Prev / Next step to the previous or next mark.
  • Look around: drag the score while it plays and the view stays where you put it. Tap Follow to return to the music. In the Ribbon, dragging up or down looks at another part of a tall line.
  • Ribbon: instead of whole pages, the score scrolls sideways one line at a time. Switch it on or off in Settings. Pause keeps the Ribbon: drag or scroll it sideways and the time follows; Play carries on from there. A seek or Prev / Next after a drag keeps your view too, until you tap Follow.
  • The green bar shows where you are in the line.

The gear button opens Settings:

  • Open Drive folder: opens this folder in Google Drive.
  • Copy Link: coming soon.
  • Lead: how long before a new line starts the page moves to it.
  • Crop width: trims the side margins so the music shows larger.
  • Ribbon: the sideways view, as above.
Editing a score (limited users)

Most singers never need this. Adding or fixing a score's marks, and saving them for everyone, is only for signed-in editors; anyone else's edits stay on their own device.

  • Mode in Settings: Play (listen and follow), Edit - Layout (place and label marks) or Edit - Sync (time marks to the music).
  • Reaction: how far before your tap a mark is timed.
  • Sync view: the view stays on the mark being timed (it never scrolls past the middle of the screen). Tap Follow to go back to the music; it glides there, paused or playing.

Full steps: how to mark up a score.

Tips

  • The app remembers your part in this browser, so next time it is ready first.
  • Tracks are saved on your device, so return visits load fast.
  • Not sure where you started? Tap |◀ (left of -5s) to go back to where you last pressed Play.
  • No sound on an iPhone? Check the volume and any Bluetooth device. The ring/silent switch no longer matters on iOS 17 and later.
  • Works on a phone or a computer in a modern browser. Nothing to install.
  • Marking up a score for everyone: see how to mark up a score.
  • Put choir dates on your own calendar: see how to add the choir calendar.
