Choir Calendar

How to put choir dates on your calendar

There are two ways:

1. Subscribe to the choir calendar (recommended). Every choir date appears on your calendar, and it stays up to date by itself when the schedule changes. You only do this once.

2. Add just one date from the Choir Schedule in Practice Mix.

The choir calendar address

https://nuuc-it.github.io/Static/pub/pmix-cal/choir.ics

Google Calendar

This is easiest on a computer. Once added, the dates also show in the Google Calendar app on your phone.

Add to Google Calendar

On an Android phone, if the button doesn't open Google Calendar, do this once on a computer - the calendar then appears on your phone.

  1. Click Add to Google Calendar and sign in to Google if asked.
  2. Click Add when Google asks whether to add the calendar.

If that doesn't work:

  1. Click Copy address above.
  2. Open calendar.google.com. On the left, next to Other calendars, click +.
  3. Choose From URL, paste the address, and click Add calendar.

iPhone, iPad or Mac (Apple Calendar)

Easiest: open this page in Safari and tap the button.

Subscribe on iPhone / Mac
  1. Tap Subscribe on iPhone / Mac.
  2. When asked, tap Subscribe.
  3. Tap Done (or Add). The choir dates now show in your Calendar app.

If the button does nothing (for example in Chrome), add it in Settings instead:

  1. Tap Copy address above.
  2. Open Settings › Apps › Calendar › Calendar Accounts (on older iPhones: Settings › Calendar › Accounts).
  3. Tap Add Account › Other › Add Subscribed Calendar.
  4. Paste the address into Server, tap Next, then Save.

Outlook

Do this once on the outlook.com website (on a computer or in your phone's browser). The dates then show in the Outlook app too.

  1. Tap Copy address above.
  2. Go to outlook.com, sign in, and open the Calendar.
  3. Choose Add calendar, then Subscribe from web.
  4. Paste the address, name it Choir Schedule-Northlake UU Church, and choose Import.

Work or school Outlook: the same steps on outlook.office.com.

Add just one date

In the app's schedule, tap + Add beside a date.

Good to know

  • Changes to the schedule can take up to a day to appear on a subscribed calendar.
  • Undecided dates (such as "12/13 or 12/27") appear on each possible date, marked "not yet confirmed". Once the date is settled, the extra one disappears.
  • To stop, delete or unsubscribe from Choir Schedule-Northlake UU Church in your calendar.
